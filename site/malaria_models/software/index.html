Software

EMOD (Epidemiological MODeling software) development is lead by the Institute for Disease Modeling (IDM), with contributions for the malaria model from the Malaria Modeling Group at Northwestern University.

OpenMalaria development is lead by the Intervention and Infectious Disease Modelling Team at The Kids Research Institute Australia/University of Western Australia, and The Disease Modelling Unit of the Swiss Tropical and Public Health Institute (Swiss TPH).

malariasimulation development is lead by Imperial College London.
